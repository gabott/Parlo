# Parlo Increment 6 — Lesson 1 Player and Completion

## Outcome

Increment 6 turns the approved Lesson 1 content into the production template for later Unit 1 lessons. The lesson is an orchestrated, resumable sequence rather than a long preview page.

## Delivered

- Eight-step lesson player with previous/continue navigation and focus movement.
- Safe IndexedDB resume boundary at the current lesson step.
- Durable lesson-completed state that survives refresh and revisit.
- Progressive dialogue support and optional Sofia/Ira participation.
- Scored `bonjour`/`bonne journée` and `bonsoir`/`bonne soirée` contrasts.
- Phrase-group pronunciation models with normal and learner-speed audio.
- Four context choices, four listening contrasts, and three non-drag builders.
- Delayed no-hint typed recall after intervening practice.
- Unseen evening café transfer challenge.
- Two local, disposable speaking recordings with structured self-review.
- Four-part exit check requiring three correct answers and successful typed recall.
- Bounded remediation and a fresh retry without erasing the earlier attempt.
- Capability-based completion summary with strengths, review guidance, and next action.

## Completion rule

Lesson 1 passes only when at least three of four exit items are correct and the typed productive-recall item succeeds. Immediate corrections remain attempt evidence; they do not rewrite earlier responses.

## Boundaries

The optional dialogue role-play never blocks completion. Audio recordings are not persisted. Mastery, scheduled review, confidence evidence, and mistake lifecycle remain Increment 7 work.

Lesson 1 is the Increment 6 production template. Lessons 2–5 are intentionally built after this checkpoint is approved, using the same player and renderer contracts.
